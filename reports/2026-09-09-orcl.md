## 5. The red-team case (final word)

### 5.1 Breaking conditions — "the backlog is real and convertible"

Stated falsifiably, checkable against Thursday's release:

- **Fails if** FY2027 Q1 revenue prints at or below the top of guidance ($19.25B) **and**
  RPO does not grow sequentially from $638.0B. That combination means the backlog is neither
  converting into revenue acceleration nor being replenished.
- **Fails if** the new 10-Q omits an updated 12-month RPO timing disclosure, **or** discloses
  one whose implied dollar figure still exceeds ~2x FY2026 revenue — that would make the
  inconsistency in 1.1 a recurring feature of the disclosure rather than a one-off.
- **Does not resolve** (rather than fails outright) **if** FCF stays negative and net debt
  climbs from $97.6B. That is precisely what Oppenheimer's 2026-09-08 note says will
  persist; positive FCF or debt reduction would contradict it.
- **Fails if** a customer-concentration disclosure puts single-customer RPO share materially
  away from ~50% — nothing in tagged XBRL breaks RPO out by customer at all.

### 5.2 Errors red-team found in this run's own work

Ranked by how much each moves a reader's picture. All four are the orchestrator's.

1. **The cross-agent RPO "reconciliation" did not hold.** The orchestrator claimed filings'
   tagged series resolved `news`'s three-figure conflict by mapping $455B to Q1 FY26. But
   $455.3B dates to 2025-08-31 — thirteen months before the article — and an article
   published 2026-09-08 saying "last quarter" would most naturally mean the most recently
   reported quarter, which is FY26 Q4 at $638.0B. **The orchestrator picked the figure that
   produced a tidy three-point mapping rather than the one matching the article's likely
   reference frame.** The corrected position is in 1.5: two of three figures map to $638.0B
   and the third looks like a reporting error — an inference, not a resolution.
2. **The 0.85 straddle-to-1SD multiplier was unjustified.** The analytical constant is
   sqrt(2/pi) ~ 0.798. Corrected 1SD is **9.30%**, not 9.9%. Red-team named this as the same
   class of error as an earlier unjustified annualisation in this project.
3. **"May be inverted" overreached at n=6**, as did calling the implied move "roughly fair."
   Both corrected in 4.2.
4. **The framing was one-sided.** The orchestrator's through-line — "earnings are
   accelerating while cash flow is deeply negative, and the gap is debt" — is accurate about
   the figures it cites, but RPO growth, margin expansion, falling SBC-as-%-of-revenue, and
   guidance sitting above tracked consensus all sit in the same dossier and went unmentioned
   in that frame. Red-team judged it close enough to CLAUDE.md's one-sided-framing line to
   name directly. **Accepted.**

Red-team also **defended** one orchestrator choice: declining to compute a 1-day option P&L
off a `close` feed stamped one session stale was correct.
